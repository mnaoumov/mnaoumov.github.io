## Writing

### Perfect Notes or My Journey to Obsidian

The long story of how I chose Obsidian. It lists the features I wanted from a note-taking app, goes through every app I used or tried from 2000 on, from Evernote to Notion and Joplin, says why each fell short, and ends with the plugins I use and how I migrated my notes.

[Post](https://mnaoumov.dev/posts/2022-05-08-perfect-notes-or-my-journey-to-obsidian/)

### How to debug Obsidian plugins

Investigate how a plugin behaves with the tools Obsidian already has. Obsidian is an Electron app, so Chrome's Developer Tools work in it: find a plugin's code by its id, step through it even without its sources, and build and reload your own plugin as you change it.

[Post](https://mnaoumov.dev/posts/2022-05-10-how-to-debug-obsidian-plugins/)
