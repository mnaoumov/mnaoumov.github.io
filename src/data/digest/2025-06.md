June was a quiet month: mostly maintenance, and one plugin that grew past its old name.

## New in existing plugins

### `Refresh Any View`: refresh more than Reading view

The plugin formerly called `Refresh Preview` now refreshes any open view without reopening it, so a Dataview query or other live content in Live Preview is brought up to date with one click on the Refresh button or one command. Automatic refresh, on file change or on a timer, can now be limited to the view types you choose.
