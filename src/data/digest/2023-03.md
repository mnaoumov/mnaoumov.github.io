March brought one new plugin from me, for renaming notes without breaking how other notes read.

## New plugins

### `Smart Rename`

Rename a note without changing how other notes read. The old title is added as an alias, and links that showed the old title keep showing it: `[[OldName]]` becomes `[[NewName|OldName]]`.

[Plugin](https://github.com/mnaoumov/obsidian-smart-rename) · [1.0.0](https://github.com/mnaoumov/obsidian-smart-rename/releases/tag/1.0.0)
