September was a small month, with one change that saves real cleanup.

## New in existing plugins

### `Custom Attachment Location`: delete attachments with their note

When you delete a note, its attachments can go with it, as long as no other note uses them, so unused images stop piling up. Collecting attachments can now also rename them by your file name pattern on the way.
