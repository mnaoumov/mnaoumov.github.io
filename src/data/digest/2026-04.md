April was a quiet month with one new plugin: forward an email and it lands in your vault as a note.

## New plugins

### `Email to Vault`

Forward emails into your vault as notes. One click in the settings creates a free mailbox address, with no server or paid service to set up; the plugin checks it and saves each email as a note with its sender, recipients, subject, body and attachments, on desktop and mobile. Mail passes through the free mail.tm service, so do not send anything sensitive. Version 2.0.0 can also read a mailbox you already have, over IMAP, on desktop.
